Controls

WASD - Movement
Space - Jump
Left Click - Place/Destroy
Right Click - Edit Mode
Wheel Click - Pick Block
1-9 / Wheel - Select Block
T - Open Chat
Esc - Pause/Menu
R - Respawn
F - Toggle Distance
G - Spawn Mob
Y - Invert Y-Axis
TAB - Player List
